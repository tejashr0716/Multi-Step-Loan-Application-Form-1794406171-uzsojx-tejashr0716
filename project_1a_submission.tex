\section{3. Underwriting \& Loan Eligibility Mathematics}
The pre-approval engine computes the monthly Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) using the standard actuarial formula:
\[
\text{EMI} = \frac{P \times r \times (1 + r)^n}{(1 + r)^n - 1}
\]
where $P$ is the principal loan amount, $r$ is the monthly interest rate ($\text{Annual Rate} / 12 / 100$), and $n$ is the loan tenure in months.

The \textbf{Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)} evaluates candidate capacity:
\[
\text{FOIR} = \left(\frac{\text{Existing EMIs} + \text{Proposed EMI}}{\text{Net Monthly Income}}\right) \times 100
\]
Applications with $\text{FOIR} \le 50\%$ and $\text{CIBIL} \ge 750$ qualify for immediate Tier-1 pre-approval offers.
